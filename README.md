                              Sentiment Analysis – Key Takeaways
1.	Neutral sentiment dominates. Indicating most users express informational or non-emotional content.
2.	Positive sentiment (~33.5%) is higher than negative sentiment (~26.7%), suggesting an overall optimistic tone in social media conversations.
3.	User activity is highest during the morning, making it the most effective time for posting content.
4.	Night-time exhibits a slight increase in negative sentiment.
5.	Instagram has the highest number of posts, making it the most active platform for user engagement.
6.	Facebook shows relatively higher positive sentiment, making it suitable for brand promotion and positive campaigns.
7.	Twitter contains a higher proportion of neutral and negative sentiments, indicating it is more discussion and opinion-driven.
8.	A significant increase in social media activity is observed after 2015, with peak engagement around 2023.
9.	Instagram and Facebook are having a higher number of negative sentiments.
10. Neutral sentiment is the most challenging to classify due to its ambiguous nature and overlap with other sentiment categories.
